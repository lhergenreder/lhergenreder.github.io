I am a researcher in experimental fluid mechanics focusing on <ins>turbulence dynamics</ins> of <ins>Newtonian</ins> and <ins>non-Newtonian fluids</ins>.

I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate at the [Department of Hydraulic and Environmental Engineering](https://www.ing.uc.cl/hidraulica-y-ambiental/) of the [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl/) under the supervision of [Dr. Wernher Brevis](https://ing.uc.cl/cuerpo-docente/wernher-brevis-valenzuela/). My doctoral research investigates how non-Newtonian, **shear-dependent rheology alters the turbulent energy cascade** in homogeneous and isotropic turbulence.

I am interested in exploring fundamental questions in turbulent dynamics through the use of advanced experimental techniques, addressing challenges across environmental and engineering processes. Through my research, I have developed experimental facilities for the **generation of homogeneous and isotropic turbulence and vortex rings**, evaluated turbulent dynamics through **stereo Particle Image Velocimetry (SPIV)**, and characterized the rheological behavior of fluids by means of **rotational rheometry**.

I hold degrees in Civil Engineering and Hydraulic Engineering from the [National University of La Plata (UNLP)](https://unlp.edu.ar/) in Argentina. Prior to my doctoral studies, I worked at the [National Water Institute of Argentina (INA)](https://www.ina.gob.ar/) conducting field and laboratory acoustic velocimetry (ADCP and ADV) and numerical modeling of the hydrodynamics and sediment transport of large rivers.
